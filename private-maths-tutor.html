GCSE & A-Level Maths Tuition for Ambitious Students

Tuition is tailored to every major exam board, including Edexcel, AQA, OCR, Cambridge IGCSE and International A-Levels.

GCSE & IGCSE Maths Tuition (Years 10–11)

Focus: Mastering higher-tier multi-step problems, rigorous algebra and mark-scheme precision, building the depth needed for a secure Grade 8 or 9.

Qualifications: GCSE / IGCSE Mathematics (Higher) and GCSE Further Mathematics / Level 2 Further Maths.

A-Level Maths & Further Maths Tuition (Years 12–13)

Focus: Conceptual mastery across pure maths, mechanics and statistics, with the rigorous problem-solving behind A and A* grades.

Qualifications: A-Level Mathematics and A-Level Further Mathematics, including International A-Levels.

  • Aiming for the top grades: stretch beyond the classroom pace towards Grade 9 or A*.
  • Building towards a maths-heavy degree: a strong foundation for competitive STEM, economics and engineering courses.
  • Closing gaps before they cost marks: diagnose and fix weak topics early, rather than in the final weeks before exams.

Frequently Asked Questions

Every lesson on this programme is taught personally by Zach Stears, a First-Class Honours graduate of the University of Cambridge and a qualified secondary mathematics teacher (PGCE, UCL).

All major boards for GCSE, IGCSE and A-Level, including Edexcel, AQA, OCR, Cambridge IGCSE and International A-Levels, for both Mathematics and Further Mathematics.
