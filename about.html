Oxbridge-Led Maths Programmes & Academic Coaching

Our Vision: More Than Just a Weekly Tutor

Traditional tuition often starts and finishes with a single 60-minute session each week. While an hour of help can answer immediate questions, lasting mathematical achievement requires a far more comprehensive approach. Zach Stears Tuition was founded on a simple principle: students achieve their best results when expert subject teaching is paired with structured revision systems, transparent progress tracking, and genuine study-skills coaching. We move beyond ad-hoc tutoring to deliver an all-in-one educational programme that fosters deep conceptual mastery, exam confidence, and lifelong academic independence.

A Welcome from Zach

Having studied at the University of Cambridge, qualified as a secondary mathematics teacher, and delivered hundreds of hours of 1-to-1 online maths sessions over the past five years, I noticed a consistent problem with traditional tuition: Too often, it is limited to a single, reactive hour each week, leaving students without the broader structure or strategies they need to study independently.

True academic breakthroughs happen when deep subject mastery is paired with clear diagnostic roadmaps, evidence-based revision techniques, and the confidence to tackle unfamiliar exam questions independently.

I founded Zach Stears Tuition to bridge that gap. Every mentor on our team is a specialist mathematician from a leading UK university, personally vetted to ensure they explain complex ideas with clarity, warmth, and patience. Alongside weekly lessons, I work directly with students on diagnostics and academic coaching—giving them the structured tools, mindset, and independent study habits they need to excel.

- Zach Stears (Founder & Lead Tutor)

What We Offer

Every programme with our team is built around a clear framework designed to ensure continuous growth both inside and outside the lesson:

  • 1. Diagnostic Planning & Bespoke Roadmaps: We begin with a 30-minute diagnostic consultation to uncover specific knowledge gaps, target misconceptions, and establish a clear learning plan tailored to your target grades and exam board.
  • 2. Specialist STEM Mentors: Every lesson is delivered by an exceptional mathematician from Oxford, Cambridge, or a top UK university. Tutors are personally interviewed and vetted under our secondary teaching standards for patience, clear communication, and subject mastery.
  • 3. Interactive Workspaces & Live Tracking: Students actively solve problems in a real-time digital whiteboard space. Families receive post-session summaries, lesson recordings, and live progress trackers to keep revision structured and measurable.
  • 4. Founder-Led Academic Coaching: Beyond subject teaching, every enrolled student receives dedicated coaching with Zach Stears, focusing on evidence-based revision techniques, exam-room strategy, and developing the independent study habits essential for long-term success.
