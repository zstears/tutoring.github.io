A Typical Session

Every student has different learning needs, so no two lessons are identical. Whether a session focuses on breaking down an unfamiliar topic, mastering exam technique, or tackling advanced stretch problems, lessons are fully tailored to what will make the biggest impact for that student.

A standard 55-minute session is often structured around the following four stages:

  • 1. Active Starter & Retrieval Practice (5–10 mins):We kick off with targeted warmup questions to reinforce foundational skills and build long-term retention.
  • 2. Homework Reflection & Concept Review (5–10 mins):Students review questions from their independent work. Because full worked solutions are provided alongside assignments, lesson time is spent addressing underlying misconceptions and reasoning rather than basic marking.
  • 3. Core Topic Mastery & Exam Application (30–35 mins): The main focus is dedicated to priority topics identified through our syllabus tracker and ongoing diagnostic feedback. The mentor models key problem-solving techniques before guiding the student through progressively challenging exam questions.
  • 4. Reflection & Independent Next Steps (5 mins): The session wraps up with a quick recap of progress made and clear, actionable focus areas for independent revision before the next lesson.

Key Stage 3 (Years 7-9)

Focus: Consolidate core numeracy, algebraic thinking, and mathematical reasoning to build early confidence and prepare smoothly for the GCSE curriculum.

Focus: Deep conceptual understanding, mastering higher-tier multi-step problems, exam technique, and mark-scheme precision across all major exam boards (Edexcel, AQA, OCR, Cambridge IGCSE).

Qualifications: A-Level Mathematics and A-Level Further Mathematics (all major exam boards: Edexcel, AQA, OCR, and International A-Levels)
